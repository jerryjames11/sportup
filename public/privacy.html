Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026  ·  Effective: June 1, 2026

Summary: SportUp collects your name, email, and phone number solely to coordinate sports events. We do not sell your data, run ads, or share it with third parties beyond what is needed to operate the app. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

1. Who We Are

SportUp ("we," "us," or "our") is a sports event coordination platform that helps people find and organize pickup games and tournaments. For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at: privacy@sportup.app

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information: Display name, email address, and (if using Google Sign-In) your Google profile photo.
  • Contact information: Phone number, collected when you join or register for an event. This is shared only with the event host for coordination purposes.
  • Event data: Events you create, join, or host, including dates, locations, and participant lists.
  • Location preferences: If you set a search location in your profile, we store that city or coordinates locally on your device.
  • Usage data: Basic analytics such as pages visited and features used, collected by our hosting provider (Vercel) in server logs.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or sensitive personal categories (health, race, religion, etc.).

3. How We Collect Data

  • Directly from you: When you create an account, create an event, or join an event.
  • From third-party sign-in: If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Automatically: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected by Vercel.

4. Why We Use Your Data (Legal Basis)

  • To provide the service (Contractual necessity): Your name, email, and phone are required to create and manage event registrations and notify hosts and participants.
  • Legitimate interests: We use server logs to monitor security and fix bugs.
  • Consent: By creating an account and joining events, you consent to the processing described in this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

5. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • With event hosts: Your name, email, and phone number are visible to the host of any event you join. This is the core purpose of the app.
  • Service providers:
    • Firebase (Google): Authentication and database storage. Firebase Privacy
    • Vercel: Hosting and serverless functions. Vercel Privacy
    • Anthropic: AI-powered text generation for event descriptions and tips. Only your prompt text is sent — no personal data. Anthropic Privacy
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your account and event data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure), which is encrypted at rest and in transit. Location preferences are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage and are not transmitted to our servers.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure. If you discover a security issue, please contact privacy@sportup.app immediately.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request.
  • Event data: Retained for 90 days after an event's date, then automatically deleted.
  • Server logs: Retained for up to 30 days by Vercel per their standard policy.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update your display name at any time in the app settings.
  • Deletion ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: Stop using the app and request data deletion at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@sportup.app. We will respond within 30 days (or sooner where required by law).

9. GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal bases for processing are contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b)), legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), and consent (Article 6(1)(a)).

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. A list of EU supervisory authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.

10. CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your CCPA rights, contact privacy@sportup.app.

11. Children's Privacy

SportUp is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

SportUp uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your preferences, session state, and event data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly create or join events. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

13. Third-Party Links

Events may include links to external venues or websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by sending an email notification. Continued use of SportUp after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, data requests, or concerns:

  • Email: privacy@sportup.app
  • Response time: Within 30 days
